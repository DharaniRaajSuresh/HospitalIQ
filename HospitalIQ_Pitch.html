HospitalIQ

Turning Healthcare Chaos into Clarity

The Problem

  • Hospitals struggle with reactive resource allocation during crises.
  • Unpredictable bed shortages lead to critical care delays.
  • Lack of unified data across states prevents effective pandemic response.
  • Emergency response times are hindered by manual triage.

Our Solution

An AI-powered command center that forecasts hospital capacity and outbreak risks in real time.

  • Bed Forecasting: Predicts shortages weeks in advance.
  • Patient Risk Scoring: ML-driven triage for rapid response.
  • Pandemic Scenarios: Disease spread modeling across 36 states.

The Tech Stack

  • Backend: High-performance FastAPI & SQLite
  • Frontend: React & Vite for interactive dashboards
  • Machine Learning: 6 integrated ML models (XGBoost, Random Forest)
  • Architecture: Fully Dockerized & scalable

Impact & Value

  • Reduces emergency response time significantly through AI triage.
  • Enables proactive government & hospital decision-making.
  • Unified intelligence for crisis-ready healthcare.
